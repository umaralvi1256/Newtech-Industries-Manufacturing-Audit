# Manufacturing Process Audit: Newtech Industries

## Project Overview
An on-site audit of a pipe manufacturing facility. The goal was to identify why the production process was slowing down and to suggest practical improvements for quality control.

## Key Actions
* **Process Review:** Conducted an on-site walkthrough to map out the manufacturing workflow.
* **Problem Identification:** Traced production defects (like salt buildup on pipes) back to manual pH control methods.
* **Process Improvement:** Recommended switching to automated pH monitoring and applying Lean principles to keep production consistent and reduce material waste.

## Business Impact
These changes help the factory run more smoothly, reduce the amount of wasted materials, and ensure the final products meet higher quality standards.

## Tools Used
* **Root Cause Analysis:** Used simple "5 Whys" and process mapping to find the source of production errors.
* **Operations Management Frameworks:** Applied Lean and Total Quality Management concepts.
